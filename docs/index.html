About the data and sources

The rank uses 5,479 days of US births, 2000-2014, 62,187,024 babies in all, or 11,350 a day. We average births by month and day, then rank the 366 dates, no. 1 (most common) to no. 366 (rarest). February 29 is ranked by its 10,467 births per leap year, about 2,617 a year over 4 years.

Pi: the first 1,000,000 digits, computed with mpmath and checked another way, searched as four digits (July 4 is 0704). Birth data: FiveThirtyEight, U.S. Births (Social Security Administration, 2000-2014), CC BY 4.0, changed: averaged by month and day, then ranked. Illustrations were made with an AI image model (Nano Banana Pro) and do not show real people.
